# Project Requirements

Requirements from the HACK-O-MED Round 1 problem statement: *designing a device that can automatically dispense medication and track patient adherence to medication schedules.*

1. **Reliable and affordable** – help people in need with a low-cost, portable machine.
2. **Storage and dispensing** – store and dispense a variety of medications, with labelled compartments or shelves to keep them organised.
3. **Reminders** – visual prompts on the display, audio cues, or SMS notifications to a caregiver or family member when it is time to take a dose.
4. **Dose tracking** – track and monitor each dose taken, so caregivers and healthcare providers can check adherence and spot patterns or problems.
5. **Easy interface** – large buttons, clear text and simple instructions.
6. **Security and safety** – secure locking, authentication, tamper-proof design, and protection against accidental overdoses or dispensing errors.
